## Pictures, undo, and links from your browser

- **Load order**: each mod's picture from Nexus, a filter by name or author, and **Undo** / **Redo** (⌘Z, ⇧⌘Z) for your changes.
- **Links from Safari and Chrome**: **Mod Manager Download** links work from your usual browser. With Starseed installed, the two apps pass each other the links for their game (Settings → Nexus Mods).
- **Updates for mods installed from files**: once linked to Nexus, Tadpole finds which version you have and checks for updates, and tells you when it had to guess.
- **Endorse and track** mods on Nexus from their window or details.
- **The game's own mod manager**: mods it installed (mod.io) are marked, and Tadpole warns about folders in the Mods folder, which can make the game reset your load order.
- **Downloads** show their progress, and any failure, in the bar at the bottom of the window.
- A **setup assistant** welcomes new users (Help → Setup Assistant… opens it again).
