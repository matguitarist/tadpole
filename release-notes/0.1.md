## First release

Tadpole manages Baldur's Gate 3 mods on macOS.

- **Load order**: turn mods on and off, drag them into order, and see requirements that are missing or load too late. Changes go straight into the game's own mod list, with a backup of the previous one.
- **Install** .pak files and .zip, .7z or .rar archives by dropping them on the window.
- **Browse Nexus** and **Collections**: find mods and collections on Nexus Mods and install them in the app (free and Premium accounts).
- **Saves**: see your saves with their picture and party, back them up and restore them.
- Mods that need the Script Extender, which doesn't exist on macOS, are marked.
- English and French.
