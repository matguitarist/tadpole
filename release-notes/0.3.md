## Profiles, requirements and saves

- **Profiles**: named load orders to switch between, one per playthrough or per collection. Your changes are kept in the profile in use. The menu is at the top left of Load Order. Collections can be installed into their own profile.
- **Requirements from Nexus**: a mod's window and details list what its Nexus page requires. Installing a mod that needs mods you don't have offers to install them along, with the author's notes (some are only needed for part of a mod).
- **Mods in your saves**: each save shows the mods it was made with, and **Use This Save's Mods** loads exactly those.
- **Backup before Play**: when your mods changed since the last game, Tadpole backs up your latest save before starting the game (Settings → Game).
- **Import and export load orders**: share yours, or load a friend's, in BG3 Mod Manager's format or as a modsettings.lsx.
- **After a game with the Script Extender**: each Script Extender mod shows whether its scripts loaded or had errors, and a notification tells you about errors or a crash. Tadpole also tells you when a Script Extender release supports your game build.
