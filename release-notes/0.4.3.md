## Tidier lists and clearer messages

- Load Order rows keep mod names readable in a narrow window: the version and author share one line, with badges under them.
- Notes under the lists wrap instead of being cut off, including the warning about folders in the Mods folder.
- Browse and Collections: pictures stay inside their frames, and the lines between rows line up.
- Updates says "No Mods to Check" or "Not Checked Yet" instead of "Everything Is Up to Date" when nothing was actually checked.
- Counts read correctly for a single mod ("1 mod · 1 loaded").
- The sidebar is wider, so its labels fit in French, and new windows open a little larger.
- Smaller fixes: aligned icons in the setup assistant, a visible check mark on the profile in use, and the Nexus API key link in Settings looks like a link.
